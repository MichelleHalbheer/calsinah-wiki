# Selûne

Selûne is the goddess of the moon, stars, navigation, navigators, wanderers, questers, seekers, and non-evil lycanthropes. She used to be a major deity, with [[Sehanine]] being her fey aspect, but following the division of races during the [[The Age of Darkness]] she now does not hold the same power as she used to. While Selûnes portfolio most notably includes the moon, Calsinah's moons are usually seen as manifestations of [[Virellaihn]]. She was often seen as more a manifestation of the purifying properties of the lunar powers, being associated with purity herself, which is also often presented in her physical forms and her artworks.
