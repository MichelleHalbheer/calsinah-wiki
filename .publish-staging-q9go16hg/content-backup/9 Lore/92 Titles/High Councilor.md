# High Councilor

## Overview

The title of High Councilor is held by elven members of the [[Fahliun|Fahliuni]] [[Council of the Union]]. By elven law, it is given to the elders of the five most important families in the [[Sialjn Territories]]. Though not strictly intended to be hereditary by the law of the Union, the elves still elect their councilmembers in a hereditary fashion.
