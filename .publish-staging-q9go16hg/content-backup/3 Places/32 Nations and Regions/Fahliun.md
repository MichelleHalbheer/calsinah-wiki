# Fahliun

Fahliun is a nation in the south of the continent of [[Niorom]]. It was formed out of the elven [[Sialjn Regency]] above ground, and the dwarven [[Kingdom of Frakrum]], below the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]], during [[The Age of Darkness]]. Today, it is ruled jointly by both races and the system established while the elves were below the surface held even after they returned to the surface, though the two species and territories became for more on par with each other after [[The Resurgency]].

## Geography

The Fahliuni nation consists of two large territories. Firstly, they control most of the
[[Thunderpeak Mountains]], due to the extent of the former dwarven [[Kingdom of Frakrum]]. Secondly, the entire area south of the Thunderpeaks, which is the territory of the former elven [[Regency of Sialjn]], is controlled by Fahliun. Both are ancestral lands of the two respective races, though other races have always been common, especially among the elves.

## Government

### Triumvirate and Prime

Fahliun is ruled by a [[Triumvirate of Fahliun|triumvirate]] designed to ensure parity between the two ruling races. It consists of the two monarchs, the dwarven [[Sapphire King - Queen | Sapphire King or Queen]], the elven monarch - known as the Arth Barvhan ([[Fey Regent]] in common), as well as a third member elected by the [[Council of the Union]] for terms of 20 years. Formally, this third member, known as the [[Prime of Fahliun]], is the head of state.

For a Prime to be elected by the [[Council of the Union]], they need a majority of votes from the councillors, as well as at least two votes from councillors of either race.

#### Current Members

The current Prime of Fahliun is [[Caerthynna Herwenys]], an elven female arcanist. She has been elected with a record 8 votes and is known for her just and effective rule and for the increasing prosperity in the confederation. At the moment, she is in her fourth term as Prime, having already served for 76 years.

The current Fey Regent is [[Pharom of the Silvershire]]. He has followed his mother, [[Shandalar of the Silvershire|Shandalar]], on the throne, when she abdicated in his favour after 564 years of rule, in 484 AFL. He is a competent ruler focused on the economic development of Fahliun, always trying - though not always succeeding - to better the lives of the Fahliri people.

The current king of the dwarves is [[Skaec Thunderstone]]. He was crowned king regent after the sudden death of his brother [[Elbreck Thunderstone]] in 803 AFL. He is known as a fickle and short-tempered ruler and is not well-liked by the guildmasters, the council members and the other members of the Triumvirate. Elbreck's son and legal heir to the throne, [[Vostor Thunderstone]] has come of age several years ago, but Skaec has yet to abdicate.

### Council of the Union

The Council of the Union consists of five members from each race. The races are free to select their councilors however they see fit, and there are big differences in the mode of election. For the dwarves, the councilors are each elected by one of the five major guilds and bear the title of [[Councilmaster]]. On the elven side, the role of [[High Councilor]] is a hereditary one from among the five most important families of the former [[Sialjn Regency]], with the elders of each family being the councillors.

### Structure of the Fahliri Nobility

The nobility in [[Fahliun]] still largely takes the structure of its predecessor nations, the [[Sialjn Regency]] and [[Kingdom of Frakrum]]. The sole additions to the peerage were the titles of [[Prime of Fahliun]], as well as [[Councilmaster]] and [[High Councilor]] for the dwarves and elves, respectively, though they are not strictly hereditary titles.

The elves of the [[Sialjn Territories]] still hold to traditional models of rule, with a strict adherence to nobility and their ties to ancient fey powers. The [[Fey Regent]] legitimises their rule mostly through the connection to their fey ancestry and thus uses this connection to bestow titles and rulership on other nobles, who legitimise their power by extension.

The dwarves of the [[Frakrum Deeps]], on the other hand, have largely grown past the rule of nobility, with the guilds holding tremendous political power. On paper, the dwarven state is a hereditary kingdom with noble houses still existing, but factually, ruling without guild approval is impossible. The guildmasters have therefore been included in the diagram of the nobility structure of [[Fahliun]], despite them technically not being nobles.

### Foreign Relations

The Fahliri state keeps close relations with the dwarven and elven nations on [[Ronash]], though relations with [[Aleanil]] have at times been tense. The relationships with the human nations north of the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]] are less close, though trade is very active. After the formation of the Fahliri nation, the isolationist tendencies of the dwarves have become less noticeable, and routes through the mountains have been established, making the Thunderpeaks passable and accessible for trade for the first time.

## Demography and Population

Fahliun is a joint nation of dwarves and elves, with the overworld [[Sialjn Territories|territory of Sialjn]] mostly being inhabited by the [[Elves]], though the dwarven population has been steadily growing since [[The Resurgency]], and the underground territory of the [[Frakrum Deeps]] being mostly [[Dwarves|dwarven]]. Many elves stayed underground with their dwarven friends even after [[The Resurgency]]. As the two races grew closer, interbreeding also became more common, forming a half-dwarven half-elven population that is interspersed with the rest of the population. Due to the close relation of the two races, there is very little prejudice against the interracial populace, other than in many other nations.

Other long-lived races, such as [[Gnomes]], are also common among the population of Fahliun. Furthermore, many other species with Fey ancestry have made a home for themselves in Fahliun. Shorter-lived races like [[Humans]] or [[Halflings]] are less common, though a few Halfling villages and caravans have formed since the Resurgency.

## Military

The [[Military of Fahliun|Fahliri military]] to this day is relatively split between elves and dwarves, since different needs arose based on combat above and below ground. A joint command structure and high command have been established, and the ranks between the two militaries have been unified. Furthermore, efforts have been made to allow the surface forces to be more effective below-ground and vice versa for the deep forces.

## Religions

The Fahliri state is organised in a secular fashion, with the freedom to worship most gods. The dwarven and elven pantheons are worshipped most commonly, though other deities also have their place in the Fahliri population. The worship of evil deities of the [[Circle of Relieve]] is frowned upon and has been outlawed.

## History

After the [[Fall of Limihn]], as the people living on [[Calsinah|Calsinah's]] surface had to adjust and many were forced underground, the elves of the [[Sialjn Regency]] sought asylum for their population with their aeons-long neighbours and reliable partners, the dwarves of the [[Kingdom of Frakrum]]. Initially sceptical, the dwarven king [[Erborlig Thunderstone]] overruled the guildmasters in an unprecedented decision and granted asylum to the elves, with a high degree of autonomy for them. Initially, some old mines were converted to homes for the elves, by royal decree, to house the new population. The Fey Regent [[Shandalar of Silvershire]] ensured that the elves would integrate into the dwarven society as much as possible, which led to the two races mixing quickly, eventually leading to the dwarven state and the elven state in exile slowly growing more intertwined. After [[The Resurgency]], the two states had already grown intertwined to such a degree that the two monarchs decided to form a new, joint nation of Fahliun. The nation has been ruled jointly ever since, with the new capital of [Kelebarath](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/kelebarath-settlement), at the foot of [[Mount Orilohn]], being constructed.
