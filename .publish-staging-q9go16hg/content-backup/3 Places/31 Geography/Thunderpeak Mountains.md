# Thunderpeak Mountains

## Overview

The Thunderpeak Mountains are a mountain range in the south of [[Niorom]], splitting the continent in two. It is the largest mountain range of Calsinah, both the longest and the highest. It is located on three convergent tectonic plates, which explains how tall the range has grown. It is home to the former dwarven nation of [[Frakrum]], which today is known as the [[Frakrum Deeps]] in [[Fahliun]]. The range is mostly impassable due to it's height and steepness, except close to the coast and a select few mountain passes. Some few dwarven tunnels lead through the mountains, facilitating land trade through the mountain range. Additionally, on the norther side there are quite a few human-made caves, that housed a lot of the northern population of [[Niorom]] during the Age of Darkness. It is known that multiple entraces to the Underdark exist in the Thunderpeaks.

The Thunderpeak Mountains are also home to Mount Aossiin, on top of which the ritual for [[The Moonstrike]] was cast by the Congregate of Ancients, ending the [[War of the Arcane]].
