# Nebaron

## Overview

Nebaron is a port city located in the south of [[Fahliun]] on the [[Straight of Storms]]. It is a hub for trade with both the human nations on the other side of the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]], as well as the elven and dwarven nations in the south, on [[Ronash]], and the westernmost overground city of [[Fahliun]].
Apart from the importance of trade in Nebaron it is most well known for its academy, the [[Nebaron Academy of Sorcery]]. As such it is one of the most important hubs of research, knowledge and arcane arts.

## Geography

Nebaron is located on the coast of the [[Straight of Storms]] on the southwestern end of [[Fahliun]]. It is well connected by road and river to the various other cities in Fahliun. Due to it's location and it being the only larger city in the west of the country it also serves as an important portal to the western hinterlands and the tips of the Thunderpeaks.

## Government

Nebaron is ruled by the Yesellejn family as [[Luminarch]]. Luminarch [[Lura Yeselljn]] is well liked by the city's population, though she often has to struggle with several powerful individuals and organizations with their own interests. Nebaron has been ruled by [[House Yesseljn]] since its founding, with them always ensuring the prosperity and safety of the people of Nebaron.

Since [[The Resurgency]] Nebaron also serves as the seat of the [[Highbloom - Highblossom]] of [[Bravajl]], the westernmost region of Fahliun. The Highbloom has moved his seat to the outskirts of the city, from their former but now abandoned seat of [[House Trylian]] when the elves came back overground, building a quite substantial palace just outside the city walls.

## Economy

Nebaron is an important trade hub connecting Fahliun to the lands beyond the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]] and the elven and dwarven nations to the south on [[Ronash]]. Additionally, Nebaron is the gateway to the western hinterlands of Fahliun and the western end of the Thunderpeak mountains, making it an important trade post for any goods flowing in that way. A large part of the trades and income of the common people of Nebaron stem from this important role.

The [[Nebaron Academy of Sorcery]] serves as a second important pillar of Nebaron's economy, with arcane creations and tools making life simpler and being a major source of export.

## History

Nebaron was founded shortly after [[The Investiture]] by [[Davrass Yesseljn]], when he wanted to create an academy of the sorcerous arts to help further his goal of studying and recording the newfound powers. After the founding it quickly established itself as an important hub for trade and under Davrass Yesseljn as Archmage the academy grew in renown as well. Less than a century after it's founding Davrass had the ambitious city walls built, which still stand today and took the city another few centuries to grow into and beyond. Over the millenia the city was a stable pillar of trade as well as arcane research and Fahliuni culture.

During [[The Second Sundering]] the city was well protected thanks to the powerful work of the arcanists at the [[Nebaron Academy of Sorcery]], who managed to raise a shield around the city, building the [[Beacon of Sanctuary]] as a linchpin. Then Luminarch [[Tison Yesseljn]] also lead the [[First Arcane Divison of Corellon]] in the war of the gods, which lead to Nebaron serving as the base of operations of a large host over extended periods of time. After the war ended Nebaron was in very good condition still, which further increased its importance in the world.

After [[Limihn]] fell and [[The Age of Darkness]] ensued the Luminarch's family did not abandon Nebaron and their charges even in the hardest times. Instead, they encouraged several faculty members of the [[Nebaron Academy of Sorcery]] to stay in the city and hold a bubble over the city, repurposing the [[Beacon of Sanctuary]] as the linchpin for yet another powerful shield. Due to this, it is one of the few cities that was never fully abandoned and thus also never fell into full disrepair. It remained an important hub for foragers who ventured out into the dark and infested lands while the winter lasted. While this time not all of the city could be saved most of the city, up to the walls, is still the same as before the [[Fall of Limihn]], though far from all of it was continuously inhabited as it was not possible to maintain such a large population with just the bubble.
