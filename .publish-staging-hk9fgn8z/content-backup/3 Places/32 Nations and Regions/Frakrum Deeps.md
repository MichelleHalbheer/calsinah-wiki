# Frakrum Deeps

## Overview

The Frakrum Deeps are the dwarven-ruled part of the Union of [[Fahliun]]. It is almost entirely located underground, below the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]], with very few settlements located just outside the mountains in mountain valleys. The deeps are still ruled in the same manner as the [[Frakrum]] kingdom before that, except for the submission to the [[Triumvirate of Fahliun]] and the [[Prime of Fahliun]] by extension. The capital of the [[Frakrum Deeps]] and thus the seat of the [[Sapphire King - Queen]] remains the Sapphire Hold, [[Khazazul]].

## Government

The [Frakrum](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/frakrum-organization) territory is formally a monarchy, though the masters of the important guilds hold tremendous political power, and their agreement is necessary for backing from the populace. Correspondingly, delegates of the five most important guilds (listed below) also take the dwarven seats on the Council of the Union, bearing the rank of Councilmaster. Formally, final say still lies with the monarch of [Frakrum](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/frakrum-organization), who is also the dwarven member of the triumvirate.

### Guilds of Frakrum

The Guilds of [Frakrum](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/frakrum-organization) hold tremendous political power within the territory of [Frakrum](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/frakrum-organization). The five most important guilds have always advised the monarch. Among the guilds, a conference of guildmasters has formed. The chair of this conference rotates among the guilds on an annual basis. This chairperson takes the title of Foremaster.

The five most important guilds of [Frakrum](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/frakrum-organization) are:

- Forgefire Guild - The guild of blacksmiths and all related trades.
- Crystalscraper Guild - The guild of miners who work on mining ore and crystal.
- Stoneshapers' Guild - The guild of miners who are responsible for constructing tunnels and civil infrastructure, as well as building mineshafts.
- Ironward Guild - The guild of soldiers and guards of dwarvenkind.
- Trinketmasters' Guild - The guild of craftspeople working on fine art, such as goldsmiths or jewellers.

Other minor guilds include:

- Goldferriers' Guild - The merchant guild.
- Alemasters' Guild - The brewers' guild.
- Alchemical Society- The alchemists' guild.
- Runeweavers' Guild - The arcanaists' guild.
- Bookmasters' Guild - The scholars and teachers' guild.

Further minor guilds exist.
