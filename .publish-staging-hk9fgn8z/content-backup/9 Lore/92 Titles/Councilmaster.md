# Councilmaster

## Overview

The dwarven members of the [[Fahliuni]] [[Council of the Union]] are called Councilmasters. The title is non-hereditary, as Councilmasters are chosen from among the leadership of the five most important guilds of the [[Frakrum]] Deeps. The title is the same, independent of the gender of its bearer.
