# The Age of Divinity

As the elemental chaos of Calsinah began to form into a more and more structured yet still wild world, deities born outside of this world started to find their way to Calsinah. As they arrived, they too started forming life from their very essences, populating Calsinah with more and more species.

And so, over the millennia, a pantheon of deities from various worlds formed, with their children demanding their space in the world. The [[Primordials]], however, did not like the essences of the new gods taking up space in their world and so decided to eradicate this threat, leading to [[the First Sundering]].
