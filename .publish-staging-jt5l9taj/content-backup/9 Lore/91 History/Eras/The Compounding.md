# The Compounding

At the beginning, there was nothing but elemental chaos. The [[Material Plane]] was a place populated by only a few worlds and fewer species. But as [[Eluan]], She Who Wanders, passed through the Material Plane, she found the place lacking interesting features for one such as her. But she had previously seen the different elemental planes and knew that with their power, she could create worlds. So she drew forth raw power from each of the elemental planes - [[Elemental Plane of Fire|Fire]], [[Elemental Plane of Water|Water]]Water, [[Elemental Plane of Earth|Earth]], [[Elemental Plane of Air|Air]], [[Elemental Plane of Ice|Ice]], [[Elemental Plane of Ash|Ash]], [[Elemental Plane of Magma|Magma]] and [[Elemental Plane of Ooze|Ooze]] - and created a world to her liking, which would later become known as Calsinah. To supply the world with the power needed to sustain it, she formed one being made of the pure elemental force, the [[Primordials]]. Calsinah was a place of pure elemental force, but Eluan had formed Calsinah before wandering on to sate her curiosity.
