# Torog

## Overview

Torog is the god of the Underdark, death, imprisonment, and torture. He is generally worshipped by jailers, torturers, as well as many creatures of the [[Underdark]]. When Torog first arrived in Calsinah, he sought to make the Underdark his home, descending into it alone. He defeated and killed the [[Primordial of Darkness]], but in doing so he was bound to the Underdark.

### Titles

- The Kind that Crawls
- The King of Darkness
