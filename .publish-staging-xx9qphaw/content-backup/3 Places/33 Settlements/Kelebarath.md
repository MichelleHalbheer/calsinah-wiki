# Kelebarath

## Overview

Kelebarath is the new capital of [[Fahliun]] following the union of the elven and dwarven nations. It is set at the foot of [[Mount Orilohn]], half inside it, half on its mountain face. The [[Vialjon valley]] outside it used to be just another mountain valley, but became thickly overgrown as the elven druids crafted the flora in and around the new capital to become a city grown out of the trees.
