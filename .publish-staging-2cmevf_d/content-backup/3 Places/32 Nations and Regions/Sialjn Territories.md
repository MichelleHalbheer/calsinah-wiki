# Sialjn Territories

## Overview

The Sialjn territories are the part of the [[Fahliun]] Union that formerly was ruled by the elves as the [[Regency of Sialjn]]. The territory has kept most of it's ruling structure from beforthe union, except for their submission to the rule of the [[Triumvirate of Fahliun]] and the [[Prime of Fahliun]] by extension.

## Government

The [Sialjn](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/sialjn-organization) territory encompasses the region of the former [Sialjn](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/sialjn-organization) regency. It has mostly kept the former political structure. The head of the [Sialjn](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/sialjn-organization) territory and member of the Triumvirate is called the Fey Regent, with its name laying claim to the elf's fey ancestry. The Fey Regent is advised by a council of the elders of the important families, though they did not hold any formal power of decision before the Union. Now the five highest-ranking elders are named High Councillors and take the elven seats on the Council of the Union. The provinces within the [Sialjn](https://www.worldanvil.com/w/calsinah-michellehalbheer/a/sialjn-organization) territory are governed by elven families, holding the title of Highlord or Highlady.
