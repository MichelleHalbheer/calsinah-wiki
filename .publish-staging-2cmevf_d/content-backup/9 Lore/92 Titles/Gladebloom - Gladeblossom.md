# Gladebloom / Gladeblossom

## Overview

Gladebloom or Gladeblossom is the title held by the rulers of smaller territories within the [[Sialjn Territories]]. It is the third-highest rank within the [[Sialjn]] peerage. The title is hereditary and bestowed by the [[Fey Regent]].
