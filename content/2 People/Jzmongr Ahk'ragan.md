# Jzmongr Ahk'ragan

Jzmongr Ahk'ragan is the current [[Archmage of the Lone Star]], the archmage of the [[Nebaron Academy of Sorcery]]. He has been raised after an exceptional career, first as one of the supreme creators of Constructs, then a brilliant research career in arcane creation and conjuration, specialized in constructs with battle applications. As archmage he is trying to broaden the scope of research at the academy and diversify the faculty and student body to be more accessible to students who are not elven or dwarven.
