# Arth Anòn

Arth Anòn, translated to Fey Port in Common, ist the former capital of the [[Sialjn Regency]] and remains the formal seat of the [[Fey Regent]], though much of their time is spent in [[Kelebarath]].

Arth Anòn is home to one of the most stable, most well-known, but also most heavily guarded accesses to the Feywild, the [[Summer Passage]].
