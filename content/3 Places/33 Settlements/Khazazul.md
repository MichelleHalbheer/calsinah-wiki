# Khazazul

Khazazul, translated to Sapphire Hold in Common, is the former capital of [[Frakrum]] and the official seat of the dwarven royal family of [[Fahliun]]. It is built deep under [[Starspire Peak]], set just underneath a large sapphire vein, giving the city its name. The sapphire vein has been illuminated by means of magic for a long time, always bathing the city in a faint blue light.
