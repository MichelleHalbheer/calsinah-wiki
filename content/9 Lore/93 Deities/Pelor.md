# Pelor

## Overview

![[20250906_1636_Pelor's Radiant Dawn_simple_compose_01k4fq6vhzfb4bfmzh79gnrn7t.jpg]]

Pelor is the god of the Sun and of agriculture. He is often seen as the creator of most things that are good. He is a much beloved deity, often worshipped by common folk and is worshipped in most communities. As most states rely heavily on their agriculture to feed the population, Pelor often has shrines or small temples in even small settlements, while drawing a large clergy in larger towns and cities, with temples dedicated to him. Pelor's priests are often in close contact with one another, even across borders, and the leadership of his clergy usually holds a lot of political sway in their respective societies.

### Titles

- The Dawnfather
- The Sun Father
- The Radiant Sun
- The Shining One
