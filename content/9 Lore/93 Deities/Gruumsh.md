# Gruumsh

Gruumsh is the patron god of orcs and the greater deity of the orc pantheon. He is named **He Who Never Sleeps** for he was said to never tire, and symbolised with an ever-open eye to represent the orc belief that he watched over them all, judging the worth of each and every one of their kind. Gruumsh is said to have created the [[Orc]] race, having the goal that orcs will eventually rule the world. Thus, he and his orcs have been waging an endless holy war to achieve this goal.
