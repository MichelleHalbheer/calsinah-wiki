# Tharizdun

## Overview

Tharizdun is the god of madness and eternal darkness. From the moment he arrived on Calsinah, he sought to destroy it and plunge it into eternal darkness. During [category:777e6173-faab-4e4c-91f6-164c5b2556fa] he created the Abyss, and converged it with the Material Plane. For this he was imprisoned by the deities of [[the High Pantheon]] and to this day he is chained within the Abyss. Tharizdun's followers seek to release him from his prison and unleash him upon Calsinah once more.

- The Chained God
- The Elder Eye
- The Eater of Worlds
