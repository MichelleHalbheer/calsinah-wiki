# The Investiture

When at the end of [[The First Sundering]] the veil between planes briefly collapsed, the land got suffused by otherworldly energy, leaving behind powerful ley lines and magically affine creatures such as the [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]] and [[Gnomes]] got invested by the energy of other realms. In most places the veil was restored by the winning gods quickly, but in some places the veil remains broken or thinned, leaving behind gateway to other planes.
