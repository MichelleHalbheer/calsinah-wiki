# High Thane

## Overview

High Thane is an old hereditary title of nobility of the dwarves of the [[Frakrum Deeps]] that is still used today. The title is usually held by members of old noble houses that de jure still rule over important strongholds. Today, however, High Thanes are mostly administrators of their strongholds, with the guildmasters holding at least as much power, if not in many cases more.
