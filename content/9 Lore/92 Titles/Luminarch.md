# Luminarch

## Overview

[[Luminarch]] is a title of the lower nobility in [[Fahliun]], which is commonly held by the rulers of important cities within the [[Sialjn Territories]]. The title is hereditary and bestowed by the [[Fey Regent]]. It is not part of the peerage and its importance is thus not directly comparable to that of other nobles, but Luminarchs generally hold a very high degree of political power and are therefore often seen to have equal importance as [[Dawnlord - Dawnlady]]
