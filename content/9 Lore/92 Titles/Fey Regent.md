# Fey Regent

## Overview

The Arth Barvhan, or Fey Regent in Common, is the former monarch of the [[Regency of Sialjn]] and is now the ruler of the [[Sialjn Territories]] in [[Fahliun]]. The holder of the title of Fey Regent is member of the [[Triumvirate of Fahliun]].
