# Petalbloom / Petalblossom

## Overview

Petalbloom or Petalblossom is the lowest rank within the [[Sialjn]] peerage and its bearers ususally rule over small areas within the territory of a [[Gladebloom - Gladeblossom]]. The title is hereditary and is bestowed by the [[Fey Regent]] or in rare instanes by the respective [[Highbloom - Highblossom]].
