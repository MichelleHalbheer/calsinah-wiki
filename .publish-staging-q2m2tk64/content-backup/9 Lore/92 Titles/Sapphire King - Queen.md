# Sapphire King / Queen

## Overview

The Sapphire King or Sapphire Queen is the monarch of the dwarves in [[Fahliun]] and ruler over the [[Frakrum Deeps]]. The current monarch is automatically the dwarven representative within the [[Triumvirate of Fahliun]]. While by law the monarch is the sovereign of Frakrum, the guilds hold a large amount of political influence, so their power is not unchecked.
