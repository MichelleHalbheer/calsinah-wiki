# Niorom

Niorom is the third largest continent of [[Calsinah]] by land area and the largest continent by population. It is home to 14 sovereign states as well as the largest mountain range on Calsinah, the [[Thunderpeak Mountains]]. As such, it also includes all ten of the highest peaks. The continent features a wide range of climates and biomes, making it highly biodiverse. It reaches from below the equator at it's southernmost point to the subarctic zone in the north.

Niorom's history is long and convoluted, having been home to some of the greatest empires across time, as well as to some central events.
