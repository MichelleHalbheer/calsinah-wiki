Welcome to the wiki of Calsinah, my high fantasy world and Dungeons &amp; Dragons campaign setting. On this site all relevant and public information is compiled in one place, so it serves as a reference for both you and me when playing at my table. Feel free to use the wiki at any point to refer to information that might be relevant to you.

Please keep in mind that not everything you as a player can learn on here is also known by your character at the table. Make sure to distinguish between what you know and what your character knows to avoid metagaming.

As of now, I do not publish session notes on here, since they generally include information that is not player facing and I do not have the time and energy to keep two sets of session notes. If one of my players wants their session notes published via this wiki I am glad to accommodate that, there is most certainly a place for it here.

Entries appear on the wiki once they become known by my players and once I deem them ready to be shared. There are a lot of dead links in the wiki, as I write my notes in Obsidian and link to notes I might want to create in the future as I go. You will just have to deal with that. Hover over a link to see a preview of where it leads and you can see which links work and which ones are just placeholders.

Feel free to browse around and explore what you can find.

Be aware that not everything is working flawlessly yet, I am still working on quite a bit of the publishing workflow but still wanted to share this with you now. If you find something that looks wrong or does not work as intended feel free to tell me, but expect things to be a bit broken still.
