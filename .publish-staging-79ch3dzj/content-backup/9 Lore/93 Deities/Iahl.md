# Iahl

## Overview

![[20250905_1031_Elven God of Progress_simple_compose_01k4cfx5kjeshsx37rk0j6s37q.jpg]]

Birth and Ascension
Iahl Lightfield was [history:06364fdc-74c4-4745-a0bd-20c96d0e2fd1] an elf in the [category:b6fa7e1d-ee56-435d-b5b4-0d3749abb327] in 729 BFL. Even in his early life, he discovered his innate magical abilities, which were soon found to be of tremendous power. He became an astute student of the arcane arts, always keeping a focus on the good he could do with his gifts, living a life of learning, progress and benevolence. Eventually, Iahl transcended the bounds of a mere mortal and [history:9ad233db-1027-4f9c-b27c-2edde9d132ff]. With this, he became the god of progress and benevolence, claiming the domains of arcana and knowledge. Despite his ascension, Iahl continued living among mortals in the physical body he was born with for many years.

Involvement in Vibrosyhn
Eventually, Iahl was called upon by a group of his followers - a group of Vibrosysh archmages who had gone into hiding due to the oppression from the Vibrosyhn Martical Court. Iahl intervened on behalf of his followers, leading to the [history:0a7e9f80-b77f-496b-a8d5-86821d38b6db], during which Iahl executed the members of the Martial Court and established himself as godking of Vibrosyhn. This began an age of progress and wealth in the Vibrosyhn empire. His extensive knowledge of the arcane arts and his extraordinary power many powerful spells and relics were created during his rule. Most notably, though, his leadership lent to the harnessing of the power of the Primordials in the Primordial Forges. This eventually led to the [history:91be7975-0da4-4b4f-abeb-36f69e93c9df], which ushered in the [history:79d63d8f-76f0-4d2a-8dd4-06109a005106].

Iahl lost his physical body in the [history:3eb1236e-167c-4328-b948-ea5378a7ae93], when Hariosylth was turned into a glass scar.
