# Ioun

## Overview

![[20250905_1742_Ioun's Radiant Wisdom_simple_compose_01k4d8kdf8fh4tanjsn1861hm4.jpg]]

Ioun is the goddess of knowledge, skill, and prophecy. Sages, seers and tacticians, as well as all who live by their knowledge and mind, often worship her. While [[9 Lore/93 Deities/Corellon]] is the patron of the arcane arts, Ioun is the patron of its study. Libraries and wizard schools are often built in her name or dedicated to her.
