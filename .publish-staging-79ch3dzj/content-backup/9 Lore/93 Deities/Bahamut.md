# Bahamut

## Overview

![[20250905_1222_Bahamut Soaring Over City_simple_compose_01k4cp92eef4ev45b1pmm5t9f6.jpg]]

Bahamut is the dragon god of justice and nobility. Known as the Dawn Dragon, he, alongside his sister [[9 Lore/93 Deities/Tiamat]], was one of the first two deities on Calsinah, born from the raw primordial power present during [category:e6cf95f9-a4d8-463c-b422-0eab663381dc]. From him metallic dragons were born to live alongside Tiamats chromatic dragon on Calsinah. During [category:88680551-2fa8-4d8c-bad6-b6ae83058eae] Bahamut sided with the new deities and their creations, against his sister and the Primordials. This earned Bahamut special reverence by the creatures born of the the new deities. To this day, Bahamut is often seen as the leader of [[The High Pantheon]], due to this fact.
