# Bahamut

![[Bahamut.png]]

Bahamut is the dragon god of justice and nobility. Known as the Dawn Dragon, he, alongside his sister [[Tiamat]], was one of the first two deities on Calsinah, born from the raw primordial power present during [[The Compounding]]. From him metallic dragons were born to live alongside Tiamats chromatic dragon on Calsinah. During [[The First Sundering]] Bahamut sided with the new deities and their creations, against his sister and the [[Primordials]]. This earned Bahamut special reverence by the creatures born of the the new deities. To this day, Bahamut is often seen as the leader of [[The High Pantheon]], due to this fact.
