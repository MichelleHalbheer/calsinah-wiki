# Tyrael

![[ChatGPT Image Sep 5, 2025, 10_31_11 PM.jpg]]

Tyrael claims dominion over civilization, being one of the major champions for mortalkind among the deities. Many judges and rulers worship him at his temples and shrines. His followers are guided by Tyraels core principles of peace and structure by strict law. Together with his lover [[Meloras]] druids, Tyrael's clerics work to keep nature and civilization in balance. The pair grows furious whenever that balance is upset, resulting in natural disasters to restore it.

Tyrael is typically depicted as a hooded figure without a face, with the symbolism of his impartiallity being at the core of any depiction of him, which is manifest through the lack of his facial features.
