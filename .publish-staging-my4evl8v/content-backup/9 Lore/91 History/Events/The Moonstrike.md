# The Moonstrike

The Moonstrike marked the culmination of the [[War of the Arcane]]. After decades of bloody war, the [[Congregate of Ancients]] decided to end the war by destroying their enemies of the [[Vibrosyhn Empire]] through a ritual casting, calling upon the power of the moon and levelling the city of [[Hariosylth]].

For this, all twelve of them gathered on [[Mount Aossiin]] to perform a ritual on a scale that has never before been seen on Calsinah.

While the casting was successful and [[Hariosylth]] was destroyed completely, leaving nothing but a vitrified track, known as the [[Scar of Hariosylth]], behind, it drained [[Limihn]] of its essence almost completely, causing its subsequent fall.
